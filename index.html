Background

I've spent over a decade in software engineering, moving from software development and senior engineering into technology leadership. Today, I work mostly with JavaScript, TypeScript, and web platforms, while staying focused on how systems age, how teams make decisions, and how architecture remains useful after the first version ships.

How I work

  • Technical direction

    I help teams make clear decisions across architecture, delivery, and product constraints.

  • Systems designed to evolve

    I care about building systems that stay understandable and adaptable as products and teams grow.

  • Teams that work well

    Good engineering needs more than good code. I focus on clarity, context, communication, and giving teams room to make good decisions.

Writing

I'm interested in writing about engineering judgment, architecture and trade-offs, technical leadership, and the broader impact of AI on software and technology. Longer notes will live on my blog.

Engineering judgment. The decisions I think about when the code has to live longer than the conversation.

Leadership. Direction, context, and the work of helping technical teams stay clear and effective.

AI and technology. How AI is changing products, software, teams, and the decisions technology leaders need to make.
